# 🎓 Student Guide - Placement Management System

Welcome to the **Placement Management System**! As a student (ROLE_STUDENT), this platform provides you with all the tools you need to manage your placement journey. 

Below is a detailed list of features and capabilities available to you in the system:

## 1. Profile Management
You have full control over your professional identity on the platform.
- **Create & View Profile:** Set up your basic information and view how recruiters will see you.
- **Update Details:** Keep your bio, CGPA, department, and contact information up to date.
- **Resume Upload:** Add a link to your latest resume (Resume URL) so placement officers and companies can review your qualifications.

## 2. Skills & Certificates
Build a strong portfolio to stand out to potential employers.
- **Add Technical Skills:** Tag yourself with relevant technical and soft skills (e.g., Java, React, Communication).
- **Manage Certificates:** Upload and list any professional certifications you have earned to validate your skills.

## 3. Job Discovery
Find the right opportunities that match your career goals.
- **View Job Postings:** Browse through a list of all active jobs posted by the placement officers.
- **Check Eligibility:** See the requirements for each job (like eligible branches, batches, and deadlines) to know which ones you qualify for.

## 4. Job Applications
Take the next step in your career by applying to companies.
- **Apply for Jobs:** Submit your profile directly for eligible job openings.
- **Track Application Status:** Keep an eye on where you stand in the process (e.g., Applied, Shortlisted, Rejected, Placed).

## 5. Interviews
Stay organized and never miss an important round.
- **View Schedules:** See the exact dates, times, and rounds (Technical, HR, Aptitude) for your upcoming interviews.
- **Interview Modes:** Check whether the interview will be Online or Offline, and view the venue or meeting link details.

---
*This guide is specifically for users with the `ROLE_STUDENT` authorization level. If you need assistance, please contact your college's Placement Officer.*
